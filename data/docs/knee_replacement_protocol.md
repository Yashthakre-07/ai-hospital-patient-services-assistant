## Section 1.0 - Clinical Indications
Total Knee Arthroplasty (TKA) is indicated for severe end-stage osteoarthritis characterized by joint space narrowing, persistent pain, and functional impairment unresponsive to non-operative management for a minimum of 3 months.

## Section 2.0 - Mandatory Pre-Operative Diagnostic Checklist
All elective knee replacement candidates must complete the following pre-operative diagnostic tests within 30 days of the scheduled surgery date:
1. Complete Blood Count (CBC) with differential
2. Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP) including renal function and electrolytes
3. 12-lead Electrocardiogram (ECG) with cardiology clearance if patient has cardiac history
4. Baseline PA and Lateral Chest X-Ray
5. Coagulation profile (PT/INR)

## Section 3.0 - Pre-Operative Medication Adjustments
Anticoagulants and NSAIDs must be discontinued prior to surgery in consultation with the prescribing physician: Aspirin held 7 days, Warfarin held 5 days with INR < 1.4 target.
