## Section 1.0 - Referral and Booking
To schedule an Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scan, the patient must possess an active, signed physician referral order submitted into the electronic health system. Patients may book via the patient portal or by calling Diagnostic Scheduling at extension 4402 with at least 24 hours advance notice.

## Section 2.0 - Safety Clearance and Metal Screening
Prior to scan confirmation, all patients must complete the Magnetic Resonance Safety Screening Questionnaire. Patients with cardiac pacemakers, neurostimulators, metallic ocular fragments, or cochlear implants require specialized clearance from the Radiology Safety Officer before entering Zone IV.

## Section 3.0 - Day of Scan Preparation
Patients undergoing abdominal or pelvic MRI with contrast must fast for 4 hours prior to the scan. Arrive 30 minutes prior to scheduled scan time for registration and changing into hospital-approved MR-safe gowns.
