## Section 1.0 - Mandatory Masking in All Healthcare Facilities
By statutory public health order, all individuals entering any licensed healthcare facility (including general corridors, administrative areas, lobbies, and clinical wards) must wear a surgical mask or N95 respirator at all times regardless of symptom status. This directive outranks all individual institution policies.
