## Section 1.0 - Scope and Purpose
This document provides updated visiting regulations for the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) to promote patient well-being and family-centered care.

## Section 2.1 - General Visiting Hours
General ICU visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM daily. A maximum of two designated visitors are permitted at the bedside simultaneously. Quiet hours are observed between 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM.

## Section 3.0 - Hygiene and Safety Requirements
All visitors must perform hand hygiene at the sanitizing station upon entering and exiting the ICU. Visitors exhibiting symptoms of respiratory illness or fever are prohibited from entering.
