## Section 1.0 - Masking Guidelines in Public Areas
Masking is optional in general hospital public corridors, cafeteria, and non-clinical waiting lobbies for non-symptomatic individuals.

## Section 2.0 - Clinical Areas
Masking is strictly recommended in all high-risk clinical areas including Bone Marrow Transplant and Oncology wards.
